# 3GPP NTN Alignment and Evolution to 6G

## 1. Standards Alignment
This framework aligns with the technical reports and standards defined by the 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP):
* **3GPP Release 17 (Rel-17):**
  * *TR 38.811:* Study on New Radio (NR) to support non-terrestrial networks.
  * *TR 38.821:* Solutions for NR to support non-terrestrial networks (NTN).
* **3GPP Release 18 (5G-Advanced NTN):**
  * Enhancements in direct satellite-to-handheld, Doppler pre-compensation at UE/gNB, and discontinuous reception (DRX) power optimization.
* **3GPP Release 19/20 (Path to 6G):**
  * AI-native air interface, sub-THz spectrum integration, and collaborative beamforming.

## 2. Frequency Bands Supported
* **S-Band (2.0 - 2.2 GHz):** Primary target for direct satellite-to-smartphone connectivity.
* **L-Band (1.5 - 1.6 GHz):** Legacy and emergency low-bitrate data.
* **Sub-THz (100 - 300 GHz):** Future 6G inter-satellite cross-links (ISL).
